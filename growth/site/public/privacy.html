What this page collects

Only what you type into the sample-batch form: work email, company, the product you need, optionally your ICP line and a note. Purpose: preparing and delivering your free sample batch, and nothing else. Legal basis: your request (pre-contractual, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR / consent by submission under nFADP).

What we do NOT do

  • No cookies. No trackers. No analytics that identify you. No third-party ad networks.
  • No sale of your data — ever, to anyone.
  • No automated decisions about you: a human reads every submission.

Where it lives & for how long

Submissions are stored encrypted in transit on Cloudflare infrastructure (KV key-value store, access-controlled). Retention: until the sample batch is delivered and the conversation ends — then deleted, unless we become business partners (then normal client records apply, with a data-processing addendum available on request).

Your rights (nFADP & GDPR)

Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection: one email to cogops.engine@gmail.com, answered within 30 days. Deletion means deletion — the record is removed from the store, not flagged.

The lead data we deliver

Our product uses public business contact data only (company websites, public directories, published professional profiles). Records are validated, sourced and timestamped. Objection/deletion requests concerning delivered records are honoured within 30 days and suppress the record permanently.

Responsible person

CogOps Group — legal entity in formation (GmbH), Switzerland. Contact: cogops.engine@gmail.com. Full identity details appear on the Impressum upon incorporation.
