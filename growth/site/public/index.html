FAQ

Straight answers

Where does the lead data come from?

Public business sources only (company sites, public directories, published professional profiles), then validated: format checks, deduplication, disposable-domain rejection, and per-record evidence. No purchased grey-market lists, no private data. Deletion requests honoured within 30 days (nDSG/GDPR).

Is this AI spam?

No. Every external action — every email you'd send, every page published — waits for a human keystroke. The AI manufactures; a human approves. That's the whole architecture, and it's why we can show you the gate.

What does "validated" mean, concretely?

Each record ships with its validation evidence: syntax-checked address, non-disposable domain, deduplicated against your list, source noted, timestamp. Records that fail any check are rejected and reported — you never pay for them.
