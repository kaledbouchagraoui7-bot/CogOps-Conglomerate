FAQ

Straight answers

What exactly is in the free sample batch?

25 validated leads for your approved target definition, each with a personal outreach opener, delivered within 48 hours as a branded HTML report plus CSV. Every record carries its validation evidence: source, checks passed, timestamp. No payment details required, no contract, no automatic conversion into anything.

What does “validated” mean, precisely?

Four checks per record: address syntax, domain quality (disposable and role-trap domains rejected), deduplication against the list you provide, and public-source provenance with timestamp. A record failing any check is reported to you as rejected and costs CHF 0.

Is the outreach copy machine-generated spam?

No. Each opener is written from researched facts about one person at one company, and a human reviews every message before delivery. Nothing external leaves our systems without a human approval keystroke — that rule applies to our own marketing as much as to your batches.

Where does the data come from, and is it lawful?

Public business sources only: company websites, public directories, published professional profiles. We operate under the Swiss nFADP and the GDPR. A data-processing addendum accompanies any contract, and deletion or objection requests are executed within 30 days — permanently.

What if a delivered lead turns out to be wrong?

Report it within 14 days and we replace it free of charge or credit it against your next invoice. Your choice. One email, no forms, no dispute process.

How fast do you answer, and who answers?

Within 1 business day, by a named person — the founder, until the team grows. There is no ticket system between you and the person responsible for your delivery.

Will programmatic SEO get my domain penalized?

Pages are generated only from your real data and only where they answer a real query; QA refuses thin content before publication. You receive a sitemap, a QA report and a fixed refresh cadence — plus full ownership of everything published for you.

Who operates this service?

CogOps Growth is the revenue company of CogOps Group, Switzerland (legal details in the imprint). The delivery platform serving you also runs our own companies every day: we sell the output of a system we depend on ourselves.
