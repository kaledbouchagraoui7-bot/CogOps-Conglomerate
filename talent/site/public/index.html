FAQ

Straight answers

Is this an accredited degree or certification?

No, and we say so everywhere: these are intensive skills tracks with capstones and certificates of completion. Employers buy them because the capstone is real, verifiable work — not because of a seal.

How is my match score computed?

An explainable TF-cosine over your declared skills against each role's weighted skill set. The report lists every matched skill, every gap, and the training bridge for each gap. No black box: you can recompute the score by hand from the report.

What happens to my skills data?

Used only to produce your scan and, with your explicit opt-in, to place you in employer shortlists (anonymized until you approve an introduction). Deletion within 30 days of request, permanently, with a deletion certificate.

Employers: when do I pay?

The monthly feed is CHF 500, cancelable monthly. The placement fee of CHF 1,500 is due only when a candidate you met through us signs a contract. No search retainer, no upfront fee per CV.

Why these four domains?

Because the 2025–2026 Europe/US and Swiss researcher-domain analyses rank them among the worst demand-to-supply gaps with real salary bands behind them (AI 9.8/10, cyber 9.1, comp-bio 7.8 with pharma as permanent buyer, RA 7.0 with ~120 CROs for the whole market). The curricula cite the same evidence you can read on this page's sources.
