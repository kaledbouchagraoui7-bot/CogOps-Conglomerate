FAQ

Straight answers

Is my sequence data safe with you?

Sequence data is not personal data, and we treat it as confidential from the first byte: transfer only after an executed NDA if you request one, storage on EU/Swiss-region infrastructure, deletion within 30 days of project close, and no use of your data for anything except your deliverable.

Who signs the scientific conclusions?

Your principal investigator or qualified scientist — never us. Every deliverable carries a mandatory human-of-record field. We supply computed, auditable evidence; the scientific call is yours by law and by design.

How are your statistics validated?

Our Welch t-test and χ² implementations are written from first principles (Numerical Recipes) and cross-validated against SciPy to below 1e-9 on published critical values. Every artifact ships with its validation record and a reproducibility pack: same inputs, same outputs, byte for byte.

What exactly is in the free QC report?

One sequence, fully analyzed: length, GC content, longest ORF on both strands, your cross-check values with PASS/FAIL gates, methods and version stamp — delivered as a branded HTML report within 48 hours. No invoice, no obligation.

What if I doubt a delivered result?

Request a re-run within 14 days. If our pipeline cannot reproduce its own artifact byte-identically, the re-run is free or the study fee is refunded, at your choice. Reproducibility is the product; this guarantee is how we prove it.
