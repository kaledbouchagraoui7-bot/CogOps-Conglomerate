2 · Together — what the combinations unlock

Pairs and chains below are implemented as internal-market tickets (published prices) — the combination is code, not a handshake.

  • Growth→Talent: career-intel briefs become the talent-careers journal vertical (distribution for Talent)
  • Talent→Growth: employer prospect lists bought at CHF 1/lead internal (Talent's sales pipeline)
  • Shared funnel: candidate signups surface employer needs and vice-versa, with consent at introduction

Verde ↔ Terra LINK LIVE

  • Alone neither can: deliver a disclosure-ready climate package — carbon books (Verde) + physical/transition risk chapter (Terra) in one CSRD-lite report with two provenance chains
  • Verde→Terra: books sharpen transition scores (published formula: adj = min(2.0, intensity×0.2))
  • Terra→Verde: screens complete the ESRS climate chapter (ticket CHF 10/screen internal)
  • Joint bundle quoted by humans; internal tickets keep both P&Ls honest

Helix → Dossia → Medtech CHAIN BY DESIGN

  • Helix QC/EDA evidence flows into Dossia's CTD modules (statistics + citations, never invented)
  • Helix surveillance feeds Dossia's pharmacovigilance screening
  • Medtech borrows Helix reproducibility packs for MDR verification sections — one life-sciences evidence chain, three invoices

Cross-cutting servants (every company can pull)

  • Sentinel: security & AI-injection review of anything any company ships
  • Capital: treasury policy + guarded sweep for any profitable company
  • Growth / Talent: leads and hires for all nine siblings at internal prices
