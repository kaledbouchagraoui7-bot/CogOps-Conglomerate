Owner: Kaled · Status queue: ordered; each item ≤ 1 hour except where noted · Rule: nothing here spends money or binds anyone before 2027-06-16 except where explicitly dated.

C1 — Reservation campaign (proof metric for weak point D) — START NOW, 1 h/week

  • 1. Target list: 40 FR B2B contacts (professional addresses, function-relevant: heads of sales/growth at SMEs 10–200 employees) + 15 CH personalized contacts. CNIL-compliant per round 3 §2.3: professional address, function-relevant purpose, identity + opt-out in every message. No DE/AT email, ever.
  • 2. Message skeleton (confident, no hedge): what you deliver, the one-number price, the deferred-start shape in one sentence: *"Service starts and is invoiced from June 2027 at the locked price; the reservation costs nothing and expires if we don't sign by then."*
  • 3. Metric ledger (append-only, in cogops-os inbox/evidence): qualified conversations, replies, reservations signed. Kill criterion (DR-0014): 0 reservations out of ≥100 qualified conversations by end of month 5 ⇒ pivot.
  • 4. Every reservation = the template in legal/reservation-agreement.md, signed electronically, stored in the vault.

    • C2 — Expert co-signer for Dossia (legality hinge) — months 1–4

  • Profile: qualified RA/pharmacovigilance/medical-writing professional (CH or EU), retired or consulting, who signs as expert of record on Dossia deliverables post-majority.
  • Ask (one page, send to 5 candidates): scope (review + sign-off per deliverable, paid per document at published internal-clique-style rates), time budget (~2 h/document), liability covered by RC Pro rider (quote at C6).
  • Metric: one signed letter of intent by end of month 4. Without it Dossia stays dormant like the other eight (DR-0013 fallback).

  • Send 3 anonymous RFQs per line (6 total) from a neutral address you control; identical scope text per line; record: quoted price, turnaround, proof/credentials offered, payment terms.

  • Use: reprice or reposition before month-8 contract templates freeze. I will not send these for you — impersonating buyers from group infrastructure would poison the evidence and risk the brand.
