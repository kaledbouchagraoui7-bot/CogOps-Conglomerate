FAQ

Straight answers

What exactly is in the free audit?

Your process mapped step by step with three columns: agent-takes / human-gate / reason, plus estimated minutes saved per month and the gate policy we'd configure. It is the same document we build from if you say yes — no second discovery call.

Can the agents act on their own at all?

Internally and reversibly, yes (drafts, computations, research). Externally, financially or irreversibly: never without a named human's approval keystroke. That is architecture, not policy — there is no code path around the gate.

What makes the assistants trustworthy?

KB-first grounding (your facts, zero-token), provider labels on every other answer, injection-battery testing by CogOps Sentinel before launch, monthly token caps you set, and cited sources or refusal for research questions.

Why should I believe this stack works?

Ten CogOps companies run on it daily — five of them in regulated domains, linked in a complete graph of internal evidence trades. Your deployment is the same machine, one tenant down. Reference deployments are linked from every page footer.
