# Deferred-Start Reservation Agreement — template v1.0 (DR-0014)
**Use:** pre-majority pre-sales instrument. **Rule of use:** ZERO consideration flows under this instrument before 2027-06-16 — no fee, no deposit, no payment data. The paid contract is a FRESH contract signed at/after majority; this document is not that contract and is not ratified into it except via the fallback clause §7.
**Status:** template; every instance requires the founder's keystroke (Type-2 gate) and lands in the vault + evidence ledger.

---

**Between** (1) [Full name, address, FR] ("the Reserver") and (2) [Client legal name, address, representative] ("the Client").

**§1 Object — reservation, not engagement.** The Client reserves a delivery slot and a locked price for the service described in Annex A (scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, price in CHF/EUR). Nothing in this agreement obliges the Reserver to perform, nor the Client to purchase, before the Service Contract of §3 is signed.

**§2 No consideration, no payment.** This reservation is granted for EUR 0 / CHF 0. No invoice is issued and no payment is collected under it. Any payment offered before 2027-06-16 is refused and returned unread.

**§3 The Service Contract.** On or after 2027-06-16, the parties sign a fresh service contract on the locked price and Annex A scope (validity §5). Signature before that date is void and of no effect. Invoicing begins only upon that signature.

**§4 Capacity disclosure (plain language).** The Reserver is currently a minor under French law and discloses that he cannot conclude the paid contract before 2027-06-16. The Client signs this reservation with full knowledge of that date. This disclosure is material and survives.

**§5 Validity & expiry.** This reservation expires on the earlier of: (a) 2027-09-30; (b) written non-renewal by either party with 14 days' notice; (c) signature of the Service Contract. On expiry without signature, both parties are free; the price lock dies.

**§6 Priority & slot.** Within validity, the Client holds priority for one concurrent delivery slot of the described scope; the Reserver publishes no claim of exclusivity beyond that.

**§7 Fallback — confirmation at majority (art. 1182 Code civil).** If, exceptionally, any obligation were found to have arisen from acts performed under this reservation before majority, the Reserver may, at majority, confirm or refuse it; confirmation must be written, express, and dated. The parties intend §3 (fresh contract) to govern, and this clause as a safety net only.

**§8 Confidentiality & publicity.** Scope and price are confidential; either party may state the existence of the reservation. Client consent for anonymized use of deliverables in the Reserver's professional portfolio is [granted / refused — tick].

**§9 Law & disputes.** French law. Amicable resolution first; courts of [Nantes] otherwise.

Signatures (electronic accepted): Reserver · Client · Date. **Annex A:** scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, locked price, start window.
