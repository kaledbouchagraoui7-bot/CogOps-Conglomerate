## §1 Charter

**Identity.** CogOps Group is a Swiss holding operating a portfolio of small domain companies on one shared AI-operated platform. One human founder holds all approval authority; agent squads execute; the platform serves. The blueprint (`../README.md`) defines the companies and exclusions; this document defines how the group runs.

**Purpose.** Compound free cash and optionality by manufacturing, operating and — when evidence demands — killing small companies, without ever risking ruin or unapproved action.

**Owner's Manual — the written canon** *[Berkshire M10]*: these principles are text, not memory. Any agent, contractor or future employee onboards by reading this file first. Candor-first reporting: mistakes appear before successes in every narrative. Rationality over institutional imperative: no action is taken "because that's what companies do."

**Non-negotiables (charter level):** deep-tech exclusion rule (blueprint) · human-gated external/financial/irreversible action · Swiss compliance guard on all investing · evidence discipline (as-of dating, traceable claims).

**Rules of topology** *[Alphabet M1; Haier M1; research/09]*
1. **Two operational layers only** (Group → subsidiary). A subsidiary may create internal sub-brands but no new holding layers. *(Danaher M1 anti-pattern defense.)*
2. **Divisions-first legal doctrine:** subsidiaries are divisions/brands of the one operating GmbH until a Phase-2 spin-out trigger fires (liability isolation, external capital, sustained profit for participation-exemption routing, human GM with equity). Swiss no-group-taxation makes premature entities pure overhead *[research/09 §2–3]*.
3. **Every subsidiary faces its market directly** — own site, own inbox, own CRM view, own P&L. Zero distance to users *[Haier M1]*. Internal work between subsidiaries is charged at notional market rates so no P&L is flattered *[Haier M1]*.
4. **The platform is pull-only** *[Alphabet M6; Haier M6]*: subsidiaries request services; the platform never pushes process, staff or "initiatives" into a subsidiary.
5. **Internal platform → external product** *[Amazon M6 — the AWS move]*: whatever the platform runs internally at production quality (gateway, factory, gate system) is a candidate product for CogOps Intelligence. The group is its own reference customer.

## §3 Decision rights & governance

**Decision typology** *[Amazon M2]* — every action in the group is classified before execution:

| Type | Definition | Authority | Latency target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Type-1 (one-way door)** | Irreversible or high-stakes: external contracts, public statements, legal acts, spend above envelope cap, launch/kill of a subsidiary, anything touching money movement or client data deletion | **Founder keystroke via the Nexus gate** (fail-closed; confirmation phrase) | Deliberate — hours to days |
| **Type-2 (two-way door)** | Reversible, inside envelope: content, code, experiments, support replies within policy, purchases under cap | Agent squad executes, logged | Minutes — never queued behind the human |
| **Type-3 (routine)** | Fully internal, reversible, negligible cost: retries, refactors, KB updates | Agent squad executes, sampled by Auditing Agent | Instant |

**Center's reserved rights** *[Berkshire M2; Alphabet M2]* — the founder/holding exclusively owns:
capital allocation & deployment ladder (§4) · subsidiary launch/kill/graduate decisions · brand & legal identity · compliance doctrine & gate configuration · CBS ownership (versioning) · platform roadmap · selection of subsidiary "CEO" roles (agent-lead designation; future human GMs).

**Subsidiary rights** — everything else: product/roadmap inside envelope · pricing inside founder-approved bands · content & channel execution · agent-role staffing proposals (from the 110-hierarchy) · vendor choice inside cap.

**Delegated spend caps** *[Constellation M2]* — *(defaults, DR-tunable)*: Stage-1 subsidiary: CHF 100/action · Stage-2: CHF 250/action · Stage-3+: CHF 500/action or monthly envelope, whichever binds. Above cap ⇒ automatic Type-1 escalation. Caps rise with contribution history (budget credibility *[Haier M2]*).

**Decision ledger:** every Type-1 decision is recorded append-only: date, classification, evidence pack, decision, outcome-review date. The ledger is the group's institutional memory and the Auditing Agent's sample frame *[JARVIS DNA: nexus_gate.py]*.

**RACI (group level):** Founder = A (accountable) on all Type-1; squad-lead agent role = R; Auditing Agent = C (consulted/reviewer); platform = I. No decision has two A's *[Amazon M5 — single-threaded ownership]*.

## §4 Capital doctrine

**Deployment ladder** *[Berkshire M3]* — surplus cash deploys strictly in order, each rung requiring evidence it beats the next:
1. **Cash fortress:** hold ≥ 6 months of group fixed costs, always *(Berkshire's $30B rule, scaled to relative)*.
2. **Reinvest in operating subsidiaries** that clear their stage gates (best-certainty returns).
3. **Launch new subsidiaries** through the Stage-0/1 funnel (§5) — tranche-funded, never budget-entitled.
4. **Marketable assets** via the existing compliance-guarded sweep (30% of net profit → Pillar 3a + ETF DCA; guard: no leverage/derivatives/shorts, STK+CASH only, 180-day FIFO holding, ≤5× turnover with fresh-capital base, one-cent floor, no ceiling).
5. **Phase-2 acquisitions** *(dormant doctrine, activated by DR when fortress+portfolio permit)*: buy tiny profitable niche software/services at FCF-hurdle prices, deploy CBS *[Constellation M3/M4; Danaher M3; Tiny M3]*. Never pay for synergies.

**Scoreboard** *[Constellation M3/M8]*: group success = **free cash per company-month** + owner-equity growth. Not revenue, not headcount, not "users". Every subsidiary reports contribution (revenue − direct costs − platform charge); the group reports FCF after fortress top-up.

**No cross-subsidy fog** *[synthesis anti-pattern #1]*: a loss-making Stage-3+ subsidiary consumes its envelope only; envelopes are never silently topped up — replenishment is a Type-1 decision with evidence.

**Zero holding debt** *[synthesis anti-pattern #8]* — invariant I1.

## §5 Subsidiary lifecycle

**The pipeline** *[Studios M4 canonical; Alphabet M4 gates; Amazon M4 entry]*:

```
STAGE 0        STAGE 1         STAGE 2        STAGE 3         STAGE 4          STAGE 5
Screen    →    Validate   →    Launch    →    Operate    →    Scale       →    Graduate / Exit / Kill
idea/dan       evidence        MVP live      contribution     reinvest,        spin-out GmbH ·
PR/FAQ         market test     first paid    cadence full     optional ext.    trade sale ·
               (users pay?)    user                            capital        orderly kill
```

| Stage | Entry gate | Envelope *(defaults)* | Time-box | Exit criteria (to next stage) | Kill criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0 Screen** | Idea enters funnel (from Growth signals, Talent gap data, founder) with a one-page "dan" (user need + who pays) *[Haier M4]* | CHF 0 (agent time) | ≤ 1 week | PR/FAQ drafted (Amazon working-backwards) + screening score ≥ threshold: market evidence, fit-to-platform, exclusion-rule clear | Screen fails → archived with reason |
| **1 Validate** | PR/FAQ approved (Type-1) | ≤ CHF 500 total | ≤ 4 weeks | **Evidence metrics**: ≥ 100 waitlist signups OR ≥ 3 pre-orders/LOIs OR pilot paid ≥ CHF 200 *(evidence, not enthusiasm — Studios M8)* | No evidence by time-box → kill, logged as WIN with data |
| **2 Launch** | Validation evidence reviewed (Type-1 launch decision) | ≤ CHF 2,000 total | ≤ 8 weeks | ≥ 1 unrelated paying customer + delivery verified (CBS verification protocol) + input metrics baseline set | Zero paying users by time-box → kill review (Type-1) |
| **3 Operate** | Launch complete | ≤ CHF 500/month base + ≤ 10% of revenue reinvest | 6 months to contribution-positive | Contribution-positive month, 3 input metrics owned & reviewed weekly, cadence (§8) running | Contribution-negative at month 6, or 2 consecutive quarters below metric floors → kill/spin-down decision (Type-1) |
| **4 Scale** | Contribution-positive + CBS audit pass | Reinvestment ladder: up to 50% of contribution, capped per quarter | — | Spin-out trigger fires (research/09 §3) or strategic exit offer | Market collapse → wind-down plan, client-continuity first |
| **5 Graduate/Exit/Kill** | Type-1 with full evidence pack | wind-down budget only | — | Entity spin-out (GmbH), trade sale, or orderly kill | — |

**Lifecycle rules:**
- **Build slots are the scarce resource** *[Studios M3 adapt]*: ≤ 2–3 active Stage-0→2 incubations per quarter. The idea funnel is unlimited; building is rationed.
- **Kill is a win** *[Alphabet M11 — X kill-bonuses]*: every kill produces a decision-ledger entry with evidence and learnings; the squad that proposed a well-evidenced kill earns budget credibility. Kill-rate is a reviewed portfolio health metric (§9) — a portfolio that never kills is failing the funnel.
- **External capital** is a Stage-4 option only *[Alphabet M3 — Waymo/Isomorphic pattern]*, always Type-1, never before contribution-positive.
- **Graduation is bidirectional** *[Alphabet M4]*: a subsidiary may be folded back into another (merge) when strategic gravity says so; assets (domain, KB, agents) return to the platform pool on kill.

## §6 The Autonomy Contract

Written, standing, applies to every subsidiary from Stage 2 on *[Berkshire M5; Tiny M5; Haier M2/M5]*:

**The center NEVER:** intervenes in product/roadmap decisions inside envelope · demands synergies · rotates squad roles without a gate decision · inserts platform processes not pulled by the subsidiary · tops up envelopes silently · overrides pricing inside approved bands.

**The subsidiary NEVER:** obligates the holding beyond its envelope (no contract, debt or commitment above cap) · acts externally/irreversibly without its gate classification honored · reports without the SPQP scorecard · touches money movement or client-data deletion outside Type-1 · uses the CogOps brand outside brand standards · hides losses (candor-first is a firing offense for humans, a kill-flag for squads).

**Three rights, translated** *[Haier M2]*: each subsidiary squad holds (1) **decision rights** — Type-2/3 autonomy inside envelope; (2) **staffing rights** — proposes its agent-role composition from the 110-hierarchy (approved at formation, amended via gate); (3) **credibility rights** — contribution history raises its caps automatically per §3 schedule.

**The dan** *[Haier M5/M10]*: every subsidiary has a one-paragraph written user-need statement (its "dan") fixed at Stage 0 and amendable only by DR. Miss the dan for two consecutive quarters ⇒ mandatory kill review. Autonomy in the market, never from users.

## §8 Operating cadence

*[Amazon M7 (WBR, narratives); Danaher M7 (PD-lite); Berkshire M7 (letter)]* — total founder time budget: ≤ 2 h/week + 1 day/quarter + 2 days/year.

| Rhythm | Artifact | Producer | Consumer | Time cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | Gate queue (Type-1 requests with evidence packs); agent run logs | squads | founder (approve/refute) | minutes |
| **Weekly (WBR-lite)** | Per-subsidiary sheet: 3–5 input metrics, exceptions flagged & root-caused, funnel movement, cash position vs fortress | platform agents (AI-drafted) | founder — **exceptions-only review** | ≤ 30 min total |
| **Monthly** | Narrative memo per Stage-2+ subsidiary (six-pager style: state, evidence, mistakes first, decisions requested); group contribution P&L | squad-lead roles | founder | ≤ 2 h |
| **Quarterly** | PD-lite: 1–2 breakthrough objectives per subsidiary (catchball via gate, not meetings); portfolio review: kill-rate, build-slot allocation, envelope rebalancing, CBS version review | founder + Auditing Agent | — | 1 day |
| **Annually** | **Owner Letter**: candor-first — failures, numbers, doctrine changes, next-year objectives. Reaffirmation of invariants. Compounding audit (ladder rungs vs. actual deployment) | founder (AI-drafted, founder-signed) | public or stakeholders, at founder's discretion | 1–2 days |

## §9 Metrics philosophy

**SPQP scorecard order** *[Danaher M8 — 4P; Amazon M8 — inputs; Haier M8 — users pay]* — performance is downstream; measure upstream first:

| Layer | Subsidiary-level metrics (examples) | Review |
|---|---|---|
| **S — Squad** | task success rate; verification pass rate; gate-rejection rate (self-discipline proxy) | weekly |
| **P — Plan** | PD-objective progress; time-box adherence | monthly |
| **Q — Process** | delivery cycle time; support response time; churn of delivered artifacts | weekly |
| **P — Performance** | **user-paid value first**: paying customers, contribution CHF, recurring-revenue share, retention/churn *[Constellation M8]* | weekly/monthly |

Rules: every subsidiary owns **3–5 input metrics** with a single owner each (agent role) *[Amazon M8]*; output metrics are reported but never directly "managed"; a principle without a metric + cadence is banned from this document *[Amazon M8 — mechanisms not intentions]*.

**Portfolio health metrics** (group level): kill-rate (healthy band: some kills every quarter at Stages 1–2) · funnel conversion (idea→Stage-1→Stage-2 ratios, tracked like the studio 3% discipline) · fortress months · FCF per company-month · build-slot utilization.

## §10 Workforce — CBS, squads, and the human ladder

**CBS — the CogOps Business System** *[Danaher M10 — culture as deployable artifact]*. One written playbook, versioned like software, instantiated in every subsidiary:
- Ch. Birth: the §5 pipeline, dan-writing, PR/FAQ method, screening score.
- Ch. Build: site-factory procedure, KB grounding rules (KB-first, no fabricated claims), design system.
- Ch. Verify: FIXES-method (reproduce→fix→verify with evidence logs), selftest requirements before any release, as-of dating of every number.
- Ch. Operate: cadence artifacts (§8), scorecard production (§9), support policy, escalation classes.
- Ch. Money: gate classifications, envelope accounting, contribution P&L method, sweep interface.
- Ch. Kill: wind-down procedure, client-continuity obligations, asset return to platform pool, decision-ledger entry format.
- Ch. Law: Swiss baselines (research/09), data protection (nDSG/GDPR), per-domain regulatory flags (e.g., Dossia/Medtech human-of-record rules).

**Immersion-on-entry** *[Danaher M4]*: a squad is not "formed" until CBS + subsidiary KB + dan are loaded into its working context; a human is not hired until they have read this file + Owner's Manual section of CBS.

**Squad structure** (Stage 2+ subsidiary): one **squad-lead role** (single-threaded owner *[Amazon M5]* — accountable to founder via gates) + delivery agents + revenue agents (shared with Growth) + the standing **Auditing Agent** = the DBSL analog *[Danaher M6]*: reviews every subsidiary against CBS periodically, reports findings to the founder, coaches via recommendations, **never executes inside the subsidiary** (separation of powers).

**Human ladder** *[Studios M9 — CEO-at-spin-out; Amazon M9 — Bar Raiser]*: humans are hired only when (a) an agent-squad ceiling is evidenced in the scorecard, or (b) a spin-out/human-of-record requirement fires (Dossia/Medtech signatories; entity directors). First hires per subsidiary follow the studio pattern: a GM/CEO-profile with equity participation (Phase-2, GmbH-level). Bar-Raiser interviewing is adopted verbatim at first human hire. Until then the headcount rule: *every task must first fail the question "can a squad + CBS do this?"* *[Levelsio M9]*.

## §11 Risk management & gates

- **Fail-closed by construction** *[JARVIS DNA; Berkshire M11]*: gate unavailable ⇒ no Type-1 action proceeds. Period.
- **Gate levels:** G1 notify (executed, visible in weekly) · G2 approve (Type-1 standard: evidence pack + keystroke) · G3 dual-confirm (Type-1 + irreversible/financial/legal: keystroke + confirmation phrase — existing Nexus mechanism).
- **Pre-mortems** live inside every PR/FAQ (§5 Stage-0): "why did this fail in 12 months?" answered before build *[Amazon M11]*.
- **Tranche caps bound every bet** *[Studios M11]*; fortress makes all kills voluntary *[Levelsio M11; Berkshire M11]*.
- **Contagion walls:** legal separation at Phase-2 (liability), brand standards (reputation), no cross-guarantees ever (financial), per-subsidiary data isolation in CRM (data).
- **Escalation on breach** *[Danaher M11 — PSP]*: any invariant breach (§14) freezes the affected subsidiary's Type-2 rights until the founder reviews — the machine stops, it does not limp.

## §12 Compounding

Flywheel *[Berkshire M12; Constellation M12; Levelsio M12]*:
```
subsidiary contribution → fortress top-up → ladder (§4) →
  reinvest best subsidiary / launch next (funnel) / sweep to guarded portfolio /
  (Phase-2) acquire → more contribution
platform thickens with every company (factory patterns, KBs, media audience, CBS chapters)
→ next company launches cheaper & into pre-built distribution → faster contribution
```
Graduation mechanics: spin-out triggers = liability isolation need · external capital · sustained profit (participation-exemption routing) · human GM with equity *[Constellation M1/Alphabet M4; research/09 §3]*. The holding retains ≥ 10% (exemption threshold) of every spun entity by default.

## §15 Change control

1. **Versioning:** this document is semantic-versioned (v1.0). Any §-change = minor; any invariant change = major + mandatory evidence pack.
2. **Decision Records (DR):** amendments require DR-{n}: date · trigger · evidence · affected sections · decision · founder keystroke (G3). DRs live append-only in `structure/decisions.md` (created at first amendment).
3. **Research corpus is append-only:** case files may gain addenda (new as-of data) but are never silently rewritten — the audit trail of *what we knew when* is itself an asset.
4. **Annual reaffirmation:** the Owner Letter (§8) closes with an explicit reaffirmation (or DR-backed amendment) of §14.
5. **Anti-snowball protocol** (methodology §4): before any future session edits this structure: re-read §14 + the adoption ledger; verify any number being changed against its cited source; if a fact cannot be re-verified, mark it *(unverified)* rather than propagating it.
