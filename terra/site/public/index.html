FAQ

Straight answers

Can I use your screens for insurance pricing or regulatory capital?

No — and we will say so in writing on the artifact. Screening-grade v0 supports triage and disclosure drafts. Regulated uses need licensed hazard models and actuarial sign-off; we point you to that boundary instead of crossing it.

What exactly is the physical-risk score?

A deterministic 0–10 exposure score: region-class hazard weights (heat, flood, storm, drought, landslide) times your site's exposure multiplier (coastal, riverine, urban-heat, inland), with the dominant hazard named and the value-at-risk proxy computed from your stated asset value. The full weight table ships with every screen.

Why at least five sources per brief?

Because a climate claim with two sources is a press release. Our briefs cite a minimum of five as-of-dated sources or the engine refuses to generate them — the rule is enforced in code, not in marketing.

Are you an ESG rating agency?

No. Ratings imply a regulated opinion function we deliberately do not perform. We sell screens, packs and cited research; the judgment remains yours, documented in your own framework.

How does the Verde link work in practice?

For integrated climate disclosure: Verde computes your carbon books, Terra supplies physical/transition screens; each buys the other's output through the group's internal market at published prices, and your report arrives as one document with both provenance chains intact.
