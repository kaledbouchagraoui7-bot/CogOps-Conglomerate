# CogOps Terra — Offer, Sales Routine & Delivery Procedures

> Landing live: https://cogops-terra.pages.dev · Engines: `../cogops-os/companies/terra.py` · Governance: DR-0008 · **Pair link with Verde: §7.**

## 1. ICP
**Primary:** asset owners & managers (real estate, SME lending books, family offices) needing climate-risk triage for committees and disclosure drafts.
**Secondary:** insurers/reinsurers wanting *screening-grade* pre-triage before actuarial work (boundary stated in writing).
**Disqualified:** anyone wanting an ESG rating, insured-risk pricing, or scientific hazard certification — refused in writing (model-grade doctrine).

## 2. Offer ladder
| Rung | Offer | Price |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Free one-asset physical screen (heuristic v0, labeled), 48 h | CHF 0 |
| 1 | Per-asset screen | CHF 150 |
| 2 | Portfolio risk pack (screens + rollup + methods + version stamps) | CHF 2,400 |
| 3 | Transition scoring per sector profile | CHF 600 |
| 4 | Transition watch (monthly rescore + delta) | CHF 300/month |
| 5 | ESG/climate brief (≥5 cited sources, enforced in code) | CHF 90 |

Model-grade doctrine on every artifact: screening-grade heuristic v0, documented weights, upgrade path to licensed hazard layers; **not a rating agency, not insured-risk assessment**.

## 3. Sales routine (weekly quota)
- 10 touches/week (asset managers, REIT analysts, sustainability officers at banks) prospected via Growth tickets.
- 4 free screens/week cap. Follow-ups +3/+7 with one extra asset screened free.
- Content engine: sister-digest briefs published via Growth's media network (internal price) = inbound.

## 4. Delivery procedures
1. Intake: asset list (location class, exposure tag, value) or sector profile (human agrees inputs in writing).
2. Agents: `physical_risk` / `transition_risk` / `portfolio_carbon_overlay` (with Verde books) / `esg_brief` (≥5 sources enforced).
3. Human review: model-grade paragraph + limitations read before delivery (always for packs; sampled for screens).
4. Deliverable: branded pack (version hash, weight tables, formula lines). 48 h screen / 5 days pack.

## 5. Handover & compliance
Asset data confidential, deleted ≤30 days post-project or per watch subscription; group Terms v1.0; QR-bill net 14.

## 6. Validation (DR-0008): 90-day window — 100 signups OR 3 pre-orders OR CHF 200 paid; else KILL_WIN review.

## 7. The Verde link (climate pair)
- `portfolio_carbon_overlay`: buys carbon books FROM Verde via internal ticket (CHF 8/book); intensity adjustment formula published on the artifact (adj = min(2.0, intensity×0.2)).
- Verde's `csrd_full` buys Terra screens for the CSRD climate chapter — the joint disclosure package is the pair's flagship offer.
- Cross-links live on both sites; joint pricing for the integrated package: books + screens + one report = CHF 1,200 + 8×books + 10×screens at external prices, bundled at 10% off (bundle quoted by humans, internal tickets unchanged).
