FAQ

Straight answers

What is in the free skeleton?

One CTD module of your choice: full ICH M4 structure with section-level TODO markers and an authoring note per gap — enough to size the work before buying the pack.

Do you write clinical claims?

No. We draft structure, statistics from supplied data, and citations from traceable sources. Clinical interpretation belongs to your qualified signatory.

Which regulators do you track?

Swissmedic, EMA and FDA signals via literature surveillance; the feed is scoped to your product area with relevance filters you approve.

How is QC scored?

Nine ALCOA+ attributes, pass/fail each, verdict PASS at ≥95% with the failing attributes listed — the checklist ships with the score.
