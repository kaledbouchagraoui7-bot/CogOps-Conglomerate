FAQ

Straight answers

What does the free snapshot check?

One read-only GET on your public URL: TLS, HSTS, CSP, framing protection, nosniff, referrer policy — scored, with the full 22-control audit offered as the next step. Nothing is probed beyond that single request.

Do you perform penetration tests?

No. Offensive testing is outside our doctrine and tooling. We score configuration and posture, test AI assistants with a fixed defensive battery, and hand ranked fixes to your team.

Who accepts the findings?

Your IT lead or accountable manager — acceptance is recorded in the report. We never claim authority over your systems.

Can you review our AI assistant before launch?

Yes: the six-probe battery plus gateway config checks (KB-first grounding, provider labels, action gates). Containment failures arrive with hardening recommendations.
