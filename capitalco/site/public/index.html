FAQ

Straight answers

Is this investment advice?

No. We deliver rule outcomes, computations and research briefs, labeled as such. Decisions, keys and approvals remain yours; the boundary is contractual (group Terms §7).

What does the free plan check return?

Each order in your supplied plan run through the Swiss guard: passes or blocked, with the exact rule and number behind each verdict. One page, no obligation.

Can you execute trades for us?

No — custody and execution stay in your account. We build and verify the engine; your approvals move money. That separation is the product.

Which guardrails are hard-coded?

No margin/leverage, no derivatives, no short selling, stocks+cash only, 180-day FIFO minimum holding, trailing turnover ≤5× with fresh-capital base, order-frequency cap, one-cent floor and no ceiling.
