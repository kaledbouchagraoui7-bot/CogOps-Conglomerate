# CogOps Verde — Offer, Sales Routine & Delivery Procedures

> Landing live: https://cogops-verde.pages.dev · Engines: `../cogops-os/companies/verde.py` · Governance: DR-0008 · **Pair link with Terra: §7.**

## 1. ICP
**Primary:** Swiss/EU SMEs (20–250 staff) hitting customer or bank ESG questionnaires without a sustainability department; fiduciaries needing carbon numbers for clients.
**Secondary:** CSRD-adjacent groups wanting VSME-proportionate drafts before scope-creep budgets.
**Disqualified:** anyone wanting an assured statement or an ESG score we sign — refused, referred to auditors.

## 2. Offer ladder
| Rung | Offer | Price |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Free scope snapshot (largest stream, full factor provenance), 48 h | CHF 0 |
| 1 | Carbon books setup (boundaries + factor mapping) | CHF 400 |
| 2 | Monthly carbon books + delta report | CHF 200/month, cancel monthly |
| 3 | CSRD-lite report (with Terra climate-risk chapter) | CHF 1,200 |
| 4 | Reduction screen (top-3 levers, priced in CHF) | CHF 300 |

Honesty boundary published: management information + assurance-ready documentation, **never an assured statement**; spend-based Scope 3 lines labeled "proxy, coarse" with upgrade path.

## 3. Sales routine (weekly quota)
- 15 touches/week (SME ops/finance leads, fiduciary networks) prospected via Growth tickets (internal price).
- 5 snapshots/week cap (protects 48 h). Follow-ups +3/+7 with a concrete addition (one extra stream computed free).
- Seasonal hook: questionnaire season (bank/ESG requests) — snapshot first, books after.

## 4. Delivery procedures
1. Intake: activity data + written scope boundaries (human, 20 min).
2. Agents: `carbon_account` (factors sourced+dated; implausible inputs rejected and reported, never silently booked).
3. Human review: factor choices + limitations section read before delivery (100% first 10 reports, then 20%).
4. Deliverable: branded report (theme accent, version hash) + method annex. 48 h snapshot / 5 business days report.
5. Books: monthly recompute + delta; factor-set updates version-stamped (comparability preserved).

## 5. Handover & compliance
Activity data business-confidential, deleted ≤30 days post-project (privacy page); group Terms v1.0; QR-bill net 14.

## 6. Validation (DR-0008): 90-day window — 100 signups OR 3 pre-orders OR CHF 200 paid; else KILL_WIN review.

## 7. The Terra link (climate pair)
- `csrd_full`: buys physical/transition screens FROM Terra via internal ticket (CHF 10/screen, DR-0008) and embeds them as report chapter 3b — one integrated CSRD-lite deliverable with both provenance chains.
- Terra's `portfolio_carbon_overlay` buys Verde books (CHF 8/book) to sharpen transition scoring.
- Both sites cross-link ("Climate pair: books meet risk"); joint pitch: disclosure-ready climate package (books + risks + limitations) in one order.
