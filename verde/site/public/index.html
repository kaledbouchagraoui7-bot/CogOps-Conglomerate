FAQ

Straight answers

Which emission factors do you use?

Combustion and grid factors from published official sets (e.g. DEFRA-class conversion factors, national grid intensities), each cited with source and as-of year on every line. Spend-based Scope 3-lite factors are coarse EEIO-style averages — every such line is labeled "proxy, coarse" in your report, with the upgrade path to a licensed factor set stated.

Is this an assured ESG rating?

No. We deliver management information and assurance-ready documentation: method, boundaries, factors, provenance, limitations. Assurance remains your auditor's job; we make their job possible instead of adversarial.

What if my auditor disagrees with a choice?

The method document ships with every report. Auditor calls are welcome; where a defensible alternative choice exists, we recompute and version the books — the version stamp shows which computation you're looking at.

Is my activity data safe?

Business-confidential from receipt: used only for your deliverables, stored on EU/Swiss-region infrastructure, deleted within 30 days of project close (or kept under your books subscription, deletable any time with a certificate).

Why "CSRD-lite"?

Proportionality. Most Swiss and EU SMEs need a credible, extensible climate disclosure now, not a 200-page exercise later. The skeleton follows ESRS logic so upgrades are additive, not rework.
