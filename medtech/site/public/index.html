FAQ

Straight answers

What does the free snapshot include?

The Annex II/III tree for your declared risk class with applicability and gap list — the exact roadmap your QA would otherwise build by hand.

Is your software a medical device?

No. Documentation and research-use tooling only; the classification question is answered in writing on every deliverable.

How do experiment trails handle corrections?

By appending: the original value stays, the correction references it, and both carry actor and timestamp. Overwrites do not exist in the trail format.

Can you prepare a conformity assessment?

No — we prepare structured documentation and evidence packs; the assessment itself is your notified body's job.
